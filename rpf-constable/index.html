RPF Constable

RPF Constable — Practical Guide (Field Experience सहित)

यह official RPF recruitment portal नहीं — independent educational guide है।

Official Notification देखें — vacancy, age, physical standards व तारीखें official notice से ही verify करें।

Selection Process (क्रम)

StageDetails
CBTGeneral Awareness, Arithmetic, Reasoning — qualifying + merit।
PET / PMTRunning, Long/High Jump (category-wise), Height-Chest standards official notice अनुसार।
Documents (DV)10वीं certificate, domicile, caste/EWS, photo-ID।
MedicalVision, fitness standards — B1 आदि official notice अनुसार।
TrainingBasic + field training — discipline, law, weapons-handling basics, duty drill।

Job Profile & Duty

  • Station, train escort, yard/post duty — roster-based।
  • Passenger safety, luggage/enquiry help, anti-theft watch।
  • Night/weekend duty संभव — Railway job की ground reality।

Salary & Allowances

Constable pay-level + DA/HRA/TA व Railway medical/pass benefits — exact figures official notice व pay-matrix से verify करें।

तैयारी कैसे करें

  • CBT के साथ-साथ running शुरू से — PET fail सबसे बड़ा risk।
  • Documents पहले से ready रखें।
  • Medical (vision/weight) पहले check कराएं।
